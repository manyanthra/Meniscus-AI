MeniscusAI 🦵🤖

AI-Powered Orthopedic Imaging, Meniscus Analysis & Implant Planning Workstation

MeniscusAI is an AI-assisted orthopedic imaging platform designed to support knee assessment and personalized implant planning. It combines medical image visualization, AI-based meniscus segmentation, quantitative anatomical analysis, osteoarthritis assessment, and implant size recommendation in a single clinical workstation.

✨ Features
🩻 Clinical Imaging – Interactive simulated 3D knee MRI visualization with slice navigation.
🤖 AI Meniscus Segmentation – Detects and segments the meniscus from knee imaging.
📊 Quantitative Analysis – Provides meniscus volume, mean thickness, and thickness range.
🦴 Knee Geometry Analysis – Measures femoral and tibial anatomical dimensions.
🔍 OA Assessment – Identifies osteoarthritis patterns and severity.
🦿 Implant Size Recommendation – Suggests an implant size based on patient-specific measurements.
📑 Patient Report Export – Generates a report containing analysis and measurements.
🧪 Clinical Workspaces – Includes AI Segmentation, Meniscus Morphology, and Thickness Analysis.

🎯 Objective

The primary goal of MeniscusAI is to provide an integrated digital workspace that can assist orthopedic professionals with efficient knee assessment, quantitative analysis, and patient-specific implant planning.

⚠️ Disclaimer

MeniscusAI is intended as a research and prototype system. Its outputs are for demonstration and decision-support purposes and should not be used as a substitute for professional medical diagnosis or clinical judgment.
